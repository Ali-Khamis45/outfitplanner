# Sprint 2.0A: basic pants

**Result: partial FAIL. The pants are built, validated and layered correctly under the v5 shirt, but they do not meet the flip gate at 4 of 6 poses, the proposed stretch limit at 4 poses, or the penetration gate at `step`.** No gate was blocked. The failures are located and explained below. The pants are not a candidate for the browser default until the crease and crotch failures are addressed.

## What was built

`outputs/garments/pants-basic-v1-mhr-neutral.glb` (84,762 vertices, 162,432 triangles). Its corrective sidecar `pants-basic-v1-mhr-neutral.correctives.json` and report `.json` sit next to it. It is generated by [generate_pants.py](../../services/garments/generate_pants.py) and designed in [pants.py](../../services/garments/pants.py), using the T-shirt's pipeline: voxel fields, surface nets, manifold repair, a smoothed clearance pass and the 2 mm shell with closed rims.

- **Waistband:** a closed ring cut 6 cm above the hip joints (y = 0.966 m), with a 0.8 cm ease at the waist growing to 2 cm at the hips.
- **Seat and thighs:** the torso and leg skin offset by 2.2 cm ease. Below the knee each leg becomes a **straight tube** around an axis fitted through the leg's cross-section centroids, radius = widest calf/knee section (7.9 cm) + 1.2 cm.
- **Crotch seam:** a 1 cm midline slab splits the legs 2 cm below the body's lowest midline point (0.792 m). Hems are cut perpendicular to each leg axis, 4 cm above the ankle joint.
- **Weights:** v2 projection transfer with discontinuity smoothing. Near the crotch seam each vertex's weights are blended with their left/right-swapped copy: 50/50 on the seam, unchanged 6 cm away (distance measured along the inner leg below the seam). Without this blend, the whole leg-to-leg transition happened across one row of seam triangles.
- **Correctives:** four hip/knee flexion morph targets per [corrective-morphs.md](../contracts/corrective-morphs.md), solved like the v5 shoulder (DQS start, regional ARAP, body collision):
  - `corr_L_hip90` and `corr_R_hip90`: ramp 20→90°. The 20° start keeps them at 0 for extension and abduction.
  - `corr_L_knee90` and `corr_R_knee90`: ramp 0→90°.

  The contract gained a per-garment target table; the format is unchanged.
- **Poses:** [leg-poses.md](../contracts/leg-poses.md), committed first. Hips are `l_upleg`/`r_upleg`, knees `l_lowleg`/`r_lowleg`, using world rest-frame axes in the `poses.ts` convention.

### Proposed max-stretch limit: 4.0 (posed/rest edge ratio, worst triangle)

- **The skin itself stretches this much.** The pants follow the body's skinning. At the 90° hip and knee bends of `step`, the MHR body's own skin edges reach 3.05×, on the outside of the bend. A limit below about 3 cannot be met by any skinned garment without simulation.
- **The garment sits further out.** On the outside of a bend the pants are 1–2.5 cm further from the joint than the skin. That lengthens their edges by roughly another 25–30% (ratio of bend radii), giving 3.05 × 1.3 ≈ 4.0.
- **Compression is not limited by this number.** The crease side compresses, down to 0.02 here and 0.16 on the body itself. That is covered by the flip and penetration gates.

## Next steps (not done)

- **Crotch:** an explicit crotch gusset panel, a diamond inserted at the seam saddle, would give the legs material to separate without stretching one row of triangles.
- **Creases:** reaching zero flips at 60–90° flexion needs either less ease at the knee and hip front (a tighter cut) or cloth simulation. The flip gate as written is unreachable for skinned loose garments at these angles.
